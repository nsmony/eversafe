You can't watch every room, every minute.

Most working families in Phnom Penh don't live under the same roof as their aging parents anymore — or if they do, they're out of the house from early morning until evening. A helper might be around, but not in every room, all the time.

A fall is usually over in less than a second. What decides how serious it becomes is what happens in the minutes right after — and if no one is watching, those minutes can stretch on far longer than they should.

"I check in by phone every evening. It only takes one bad afternoon for that not to be enough."

A scenario we hear often from families we've spoken with

Three steps, running quietly in the background.

No one has to remember to check an app. EverSafe only speaks up when something actually needs your attention.

01

Cameras watch the key rooms

Discreet CCTV cameras go where falls are most likely — the living room, the hallway, near the bathroom entrance.

02

AI detects the fall on the spot

A small Mini PC inside the home runs the detection model directly. Nothing is sent anywhere until there's something to report.

Your family is alerted in seconds

Built around the moment that matters most.

Every part of EverSafe is designed to shorten the time between a fall and someone knowing about it — without adding another thing for your family to manage.

Speed

Seconds, not minutes

Detection and alert delivery happen in real time. No one has to happen to glance at a camera feed or wait for a scheduled check-in.

Privacy

Nothing leaves the house by default

Video is analyzed on the Mini PC inside the home. Only an alert and a still image reach the family app — full footage stays local unless your family chooses to review it.

Coordination

One family, one view

Everyone in the household group sees the same alert and the same response status, so two people don't rush over while a third assumes it's handled.

Accuracy over time
